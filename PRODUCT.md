# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Thai developers, researchers, and small technical teams choosing a desk-side machine they will use and tune themselves for local AI.

## Product Purpose

Sell one configurable local AI rig through model-fit guidance, build specifications, and honest benchmark context, with Mimir Suites Local available as an option.

## Brand Personality

Geeky, exact, and approachable. The site should feel like a premium bench notebook for a serious machine.

## Anti-references

- Gaming-PC storefronts and RGB-heavy component catalogs.
- Procurement language copied from the workstation brand.
- Invented benchmark claims or unsupported vendor status.
- Creator-studio or training-course positioning.

## Design Principles

- Lead with model fit, VRAM, and measured constraints.
- Keep the desk-side rig as the single product idea.
- Write for the person who will sit at the machine.
- Use motion only to clarify the specification story.
- Make hardware-only and configured-system paths equally clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives for every animated surface.
